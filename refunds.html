Iconscape Refund Policy

Updated November 16, 2026

Iconscape has three free scenes, so you can see exactly how it works on your desktop before buying anything. If a paid scene still isn't right for you, here's how refunds work.

Within 7 days: no questions asked

If you ask within 7 days of buying, you'll get a full refund for any reason.

How to ask

Email support@iconscape.app from the address you used to buy, or include your order number (it's on your receipt from Polar).

What happens next

  • Polar, our merchant of record, refunds the full amount to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.
  • The license key from that purchase is revoked. The scenes it unlocked lock again the next time Iconscape checks the key (about once a week). Your free scenes keep working.

After 7 days

If something goes wrong later, for example a scene doesn't work properly on your Mac and we can't fix it, email us and we'll look at it case by case.

Your rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under the consumer laws where you live.

Contact

Questions? Email support@iconscape.app.
