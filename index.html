Opening it the first time

Iconscape is made by an independent developer and isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once:

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Iconscape into Applications.
  2. Open Iconscape. When macOS says it can't verify the developer, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

You only need to do this once.

Questions

Why does Iconscape need access to Finder?

Finder is the only place macOS keeps your icon positions. Iconscape asks for those positions and your icon size, nothing else. It never reads file names or contents, and nothing leaves your Mac. See the privacy policy.

Will it slow down my Mac or drain the battery?

No. The landscape is drawn once, and the moving parts are animated by macOS's own graphics system. When you're working in other apps and can't see the desktop, the animation pauses and Iconscape uses next to no CPU.

Does it work with Stacks?

Yes. With Stacks on, the landscape follows the Stacks layout along the right edge of the screen.

Can I try a paid scene before buying?

Yes. Click any locked scene and choose Try for 60 seconds. It takes over your desktop for a minute, then switches back.

I bought a scene. How do I unlock it?

Your license key arrives by email. In Iconscape, open the menu bar icon, choose Unlock All Themes… or click the scene, then paste the key and click Activate.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Iconscape isn't right for you, reply to your receipt email within 7 days of buying.

How do updates and new scenes arrive?

Iconscape checks for updates once a day and installs them with one click. New scenes arrive in updates, and if you bought every scene, new ones unlock automatically.

Is it on the Mac App Store?

No. The App Store doesn't allow apps to ask Finder where icons are, so Iconscape is available only from this site.
